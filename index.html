THROW POWER

How to play

Knock everyone over with a blaster, flying potted plants, a sniper rifle and your bare hands. Anyone who gets hit goes full ragdoll and gets back up on their own.

Play with friends

One device hosts and gets a room code and an invite link. Up to 3 friends join from anywhere, on the same Wi-Fi or across the internet (4 players max). Send them the link, or have them open the game and enter the code.

Not connected. You're playing solo.
Can't connect? Use your own relay server

The game tries a direct connection first, then its built-in relays (Metered and PeerJS). If those don't work, add a TURN server you have access to (from a TURN provider, or one you run yourself). Everyone in the game should add the same one. It's saved in this browser only.
